Pitwall Sim

I built this to drive a lap and see what seven old patents do to it. Each switch changes the car or adds a gauge. The lap table shows what it did to your time. Keyboard and touch work. Any controller your browser can see should work too, but I haven't tried it on real hardware.

The switch panel: 1958 to 2001

Seven patents, by the year each was first filed. All seven are past their full 20-year term, so none of them can come back. The numbers in this sim are illustrative, so use it to compare switches against each other, not as a real car model.

Wheel and pedals

The sim reads any controller your browser can see. Map your pedals once and it remembers them on this device. I haven't tested it on a real wheel or pad, so treat it as untested on real hardware.

What works

WorksKeyboard and touch. Any controller the browser exposes through the Gamepad API: with a standard pad, R2 is gas, L2 is brake and the left stick steers.
UntestedReal wheels and pads, and VR. The pedal-mapping wizard and the WebXR mode are written to the specs, but untested on a headset or a real wheel. If a controller doesn't show up, press a button on it first, or try Chrome or Edge.
Plain HTML and ES modules, with no account and no build step for the sim. The only library is three.js (MIT), kept in this folder. Pitwall Sim is not affiliated with any team, manufacturer or patent holder. Patent links go to Google Patents, whose status lines are estimates. Sim values are illustrative.
